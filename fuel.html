Nadia's four rules of fueling

  1. Total daily protein is the thing. Timing is the decoration. Hitting your daily number reliably matters far more than which hour you eat it in. Once the total is right, spreading it across the day gives you a small extra benefit. Getting the total wrong cannot be fixed by timing.
  2. You cannot supplement your way out of not eating. The powders on the shelf are convenient food, not a shortcut. If your daily protein comes from real meals, you may not need a scoop at all, and Nadia will happily tell you that even though it is bad for business.
  3. Carbohydrate is not the enemy of anything. It is the fuel for hard training and it is what refills the tank afterward. Cutting it makes sessions feel worse and rarely changes the outcome once calories and protein are matched.
  4. Eat enough to train, not just enough to look like you train. Chronic under-eating shows up as stalled progress, poor sleep, injuries, disrupted menstrual cycles, and bone loss. That is Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport, it affects men and women, and it is far more common than overtraining.

How the staff verify (so you don't have to take our word for it)

Sports nutrition is the most heavily marketed corner of fitness, and the gap between what a label implies and what a trial shows is where most of the money is made. Nadia carries PubMed, USDA FoodData Central and NIH ODS, and she uses them on the boring claims as well as the exciting ones.
