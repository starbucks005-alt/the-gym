The bar is not a supplement store

Everything here is food you could buy at a normal grocery store, plus one protein powder if you want it. There is nothing on this menu that requires a subscription, a coach code, or a canister with a lightning bolt on it.

The honest catch: a smoothie is a drink you can consume in ninety seconds that took a lot of food to make. That is exactly what you want after a hard session, and exactly what you do not want as a fourth snack. The macro line on every blend is there so you know which one you are having.

      Zara's four rules of the blender

      1. Load it in order. Liquid, then soft food, then greens, then frozen, then powder on top. Load it backwards and you get a powder cement layer on the blades and a motor that whines at you.
      2. Frozen fruit, not ice. Ice waters the drink down as it melts. Frozen banana and frozen berries give you the same cold and thick without diluting the flavor.
      3. Fat and fiber are the difference between a drink and a meal. A tablespoon of nut butter or seeds, plus the actual fruit fiber, is what keeps you full past the next hour. Skip both and you have a milkshake with good PR.
      4. Rinse it immediately. Water, one drop of soap, run it for ten seconds. A smoothie you have to scrub after is a smoothie you stop making.
