What the three colours actually mean

Green · checks out
The evidence supports the claim as stated. Not "someone said it works", but that the research points the same way the claim does. Green is the rarest verdict here, and that is worth noticing.
Amber · it depends
There is something real underneath, and the popular version overstates it, or it only holds under conditions people leave out. Amber is the most common verdict, because most fitness claims are a real effect with the size and the caveats stripped off.
Red · myth
The claim is wrong. Sometimes harmlessly, sometimes in a way that gets people hurt or takes their money. Red is not a matter of taste, and Eli does not negotiate it.

How a claim gets a verdict

A claim reaches Eli from somewhere on the floor. Jax finds it spiking in search, Dom debunks it in one sentence, Reece form-checks the reel, or a member simply asks. Then the person whose lane it is looks it up: Nadia on nutrition, Dr. Henry on supplements and interactions, Dr. Lena on injury, Dr. Sana on sleep and recovery, Amara on botanicals.

Eli's job is the last step, and it is a narrow one. He does not decide whether something sounds reasonable. He decides whether the evidence named actually says what the claim says, and then he writes down where he looked. If nobody has properly studied it, that is the answer, and it goes in as amber rather than being quietly upgraded.
